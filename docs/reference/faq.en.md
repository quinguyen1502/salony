# FAQ

Short answers. Detail lives on the linked page.

??? question "Do I need a login account?"

    No. The salon lives on the device, and a recovery key, not a password,
    brings it back on a new one. See
    [Protect and restore your salon](../admin/backup.md), or
    [how the recovery key works](../tech/identity.md) for the technical side.

??? question "Can Staff create appointments?"

    No. Only Owner and Manager. Staff draft invoices, set availability, and
    see their own earnings. See [Roles](roles.md).

??? question "What if the network is down?"

    Bookings, bills, and reports still run. Sync waits until the app is open
    and a path exists. See [Local-first](../tech/local-first.md).

??? question "Does Salony keep a copy of my salon?"

    No. You keep the recovery key and the salon backup yourself, from
    **Settings → Protect your salon**. See
    [Protect and restore your salon](../admin/backup.md). If you turn on
    [Online booking](../admin/online-booking.md), a public brochure and
    pending requests sit on that page until you review them — not the
    appointment book.

??? question "What if I lose my phone?"

    On a new device, tap **Restore your salon on this device**. The recovery
    key brings back ownership; the salon backup, or another synced device,
    brings back the data. You need both. See
    [Protect and restore your salon](../admin/backup.md).

??? question "Can a customer book a confirmed slot from the public page?"

    No. They send a **request**. Owner or Manager **Confirm** it in the
    app. The time on the form is a preference, not a reserved slot. See
    [Online booking](../admin/online-booking.md).

??? question "Is adding a device the same as logging into one account?"

    No. Each device joins the salon on its own. Pair from **Nearby
    devices**, or scan QR. See [Devices and sync](../admin/devices.md).

??? question "The other device does not appear nearby?"

    Open **Join a store** (the QR button on **Select store**) on that device, connect to this Wi-Fi,
    or scan its QR. A Mac or PC cannot scan; it picks from the list.
    See [Devices and sync](../admin/devices.md).

??? question "Can two devices sync if they are not on the same Wi-Fi?"

    Not in this version. They need the salon Wi-Fi. Pair nearby or with QR.
    See [Devices and sync](../admin/devices.md) and
    [Downloads](../admin/downloads.md).

??? question "Is there Bluetooth thermal printing?"

    Yes, with a printer that speaks Bluetooth Low Energy. A printer sold as
    "not compatible with iPhone" speaks the older Bluetooth Classic and
    cannot be used. See [Thermal printers](../guide/printers.md) for the models that
    work and how to check one in a minute.

??? question "Where does the AI assistant send data?"

    It does not send salon data to a cloud. The internet is used only to
    download a model. See [AI assistant](../guide/ai-assistant.md).
