## Install, pair, and go { #getting-started }

<div class="home-steps" markdown>

-   **Get the app**

    Version 1.3.0 for Android, installed straight from the file.

    [Downloads](admin/downloads.md)

-   **Create a salon, or pair**

    The owner names the salon. Staff phones pair with the salon's device:
    hold them close, or scan a QR.

    [First launch](start/first-launch.md)

-   **Welcome clients**

    Add services and staff, then book and bill. Save your recovery code.

    [First day](start/first-day.md)

## Your salon, your data { #privacy }

<div class="grid cards home-cards" markdown>

-   :material-cellphone-check:{ .lg .middle } **Data on your device**

    ---

    Bookings, bills and customers live on the salon's own phones and
    computers. Salony keeps no copy in the cloud.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Local-first](tech/local-first.md)

-   :material-signal-off:{ .lg .middle } **Works offline**

    ---

    Book, bill and check earnings as usual. Devices sync when they meet
    again.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Run the day](guide/run-the-day.md)

-   :material-shield-lock:{ .lg .middle } **End-to-end encrypted sync**

    ---

    Devices in your salon sync over the salon's Wi-Fi, encrypted from one
    device to the other. This version does not sync over the internet yet.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Devices and sync](admin/devices.md)

-   :material-key-variant:{ .lg .middle } **No sign-in**

    ---

    No email, no password. Lose a phone and you get the salon back with the
    recovery code and a backup.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Protect and recover your salon](admin/backup.md)

</div>

## How it works { #how-it-works }

<div class="home-lead" markdown>

The publisher keeps no copy of your salon: nothing to read, nothing to sell,
and nothing to restore for you. In exchange, keeping the salon safe is
yours: the recovery key and a salon backup, both in **Settings → Protect your
salon**. Lose the device with no backup and no other device in the store, and
that device's data is gone.
