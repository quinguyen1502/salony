# Run the day

Today screen: who is in service, who is next, what needs you, and who is
free.

## Overview

**Today** is the owner/manager day screen. Staff have their own Home —
*their* bookings and earnings.

Staff keep the phone layout on every device: on a tablet or computer it sits
in a phone-width column in the middle of the screen.

## Concepts

- **Stat strip** — revenue so far today, check-ins against today's
  bookings, and drafts waiting for approval. On a tablet it also shows new
  customers and how many staff are working.
- **In service** — bookings that are checked in and not completed, with the
  minutes left.
- **Next** — the earliest booking not checked in yet, with **Check in** and
  **Open booking**. A checked-in booking never shows here. If it has no
  staff, the button offers to assign someone first.
- **Needs attention** — drafts to approve, bookings without staff, and a
  backup reminder, each with its own button.
- **Staff** — who is free, who is busy, and until when. Tap someone to see
  their bookings.
- **Still to come today** — the rest of the day after Next.

On a tablet, **Today's board** shows the day as one row per staff member,
with a red line at the current time.

## Getting around

Phone: the bottom bar has **Today**, **Bookings**, a round **+** in the
middle, **Bills**, and **More**. **+** opens **New appointment** and **New
invoice**. **More** is a tab like the others. It holds:

- the store card, with **Switch store**;
- sync and backup status;
- **Operations**: Calendar, Customers, Staff, Services;
- **Money**: Earnings and payouts, Reports, Promotions;
- **Salon**: Online booking and Settings.

Each row shows a live figure, for example *2 working today* or *240K ₫
unpaid*.

Tablet / desktop: a bar along the top has the store name (tap to switch
store), **Today**, **Bookings**, **Bills**, **Schedule**, **Customers**, and
**All** for the rest. On the right are sync status and the **Booking** and
**Bill** buttons. A narrow window, or a tablet held upright, shows three tabs
plus **All**. Back (or Escape) closes an open record on the right first.

Dark appearance: **Settings → Appearance → Dark** (app shots invert against
the docs theme).
